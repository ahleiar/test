## Capture

- Film horizontally in 4K or 1080p, at 24 or 30 fps, with the lens at eye level. Keep some space around your head and shoulders for reframing.
- Use a quiet room and soft window light. Avoid a bright window behind you; lock focus and exposure if the phone allows it.
- Leave about three seconds before and after each take. Keep the original files; avoid sending a re-compressed messaging-app copy.
- For the cleanest script match, you may record Acts 2 and 3 as voice-only pickups too. The existing track is a reference, not a word-verified final narration.
