ACT 1 — INTRODUCTION AND BOTTOM LINE

One word I would use to describe myself is cosmopolitan—which in Greek means a citizen of the world.

Hi, I’m Alexander Leiar, a third-year business student at the University of Arizona’s Eller College of Management. For leaders in commercial real estate and entrepreneurship, I bring an international perspective, the ability to adapt, and a genuine interest in building relationships.

My story is a little different from most. I was born in Portugal and grew up in South Africa, where I was exposed to different cultures, people, and ways of life from an early age. That experience taught me to stay open-minded and make the most of new situations.

ACT 2 — SPORTS AND TRAVEL

Growing up, sports were a big part of my life. I played rugby and water polo and swam competitively. Those experiences taught me discipline, resilience, and how to perform both as part of a team and on my own.

Traveling across Africa, Europe, and Asia also pushed me outside my comfort zone. Each place introduced me to new perspectives and helped me become more comfortable connecting with people from different backgrounds.

ACT 3 — PROFESSIONAL EXPERIENCE

That adaptability has carried into my professional experiences. At Fine & Rare Wines in South Africa, I gained exposure to supply chain operations and client service. At DM Financial in Ireland, I worked with financial statements, helped with payroll and supported the management accounts team.

Most recently, at Abstrakt Marketing Group in St. Louis, I stepped into a fast-paced sales environment and connected with business leaders to identify opportunities. There, I generated 10 qualified appointments representing roughly $250,000 in potential pipeline revenue.

Each experience challenged me in a different way, but all three taught me the importance of getting comfortable being uncomfortable, building trust, and being willing to learn.

ACT 4 — WHAT I BRING

Today, I bring those lessons with me to Eller and to the Arizona water polo team. Balancing academics, athletics, and professional opportunities has strengthened my time management and commitment to the people who count on me.

For a real estate team or growing business, I bring a willingness to learn, a perspective shaped by international experiences, and the communication skills to connect with different people. I’m excited by opportunities where I can help build relationships, solve problems, and contribute to a team.
